Today marked the beginning of the CEAMLS Symposium Weekend, and it was an exciting opportunity to see the incredible work being done by students from different research groups. Walking through the symposium, I met many interesting people and learned about a wide variety of creative and innovative projects. It was inspiring to see how each team approached different research challenges and communicated their findings in unique ways.

It was great connecting with them, learning more about their company, and sharing our connection to New York. Conversations like these reminded me how valuable networking can be and how research events create opportunities to build relationships with professionals.

We also attended several poster sessions throughout the day. Listening to participants explain their research gave me new perspectives on different scientific and engineering topics while also providing ideas for improving the way I communicate my own work.
